About this track

The Social Science track is one of the five tracks of INSIGHT 5.0: Science, Media, Literature, Social Science and Technology. Together they explore the intersection of intelligence, innovation and human values.

The conference aims to foster interdisciplinary dialogue, knowledge sharing, and collaborative research to address emerging global challenges.

Abstracts and full papers for this track are submitted through the conference forms below.
